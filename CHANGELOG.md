## [0.1.0] — 2026-10-26

First public playable demo: **“The Fallen Gate”** (about 10–15 minutes).

### Added

- **Lune** — run, jump (variable height), wall-jump, invincible dash
- 3-hit glaive combo, up/down attacks, down-attack bounce, parry
- Hold-to-heal with Starlight
- **Gravity Core** — flip gravity and walk on the ceiling to cross the spike pit
- Enemies: Husk (patrol + claw lunge) and Wisp (flying charge)
- Boss: **The Gatewarden** — slam, charge and leap shockwaves; phase 2 adds Star Rain
- Starwell checkpoints, Stardust pickups, and Echo (recovers your Stardust after death)
- Two music loops, placeholder SFX, gamepad rumble
- Full keyboard and Xbox-layout gamepad support
- `play.bat` launcher that reassembles the split executable on first run

### Known limitations

- Placeholder art, audio and music
- Windows x64 only
- No save system — progress lasts for the session only
