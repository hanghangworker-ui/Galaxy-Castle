# Galaxy Castle — playable demo v0.4 “The Fallen Gate”

Second public build. Everything from v0.1, plus a proper front end: a title screen, three save slots, settings with key rebinding, a pause menu, and camera zones that hold the right frame in every room.

## What's new in v0.4

- **Main menu** — Continue, New Game, Load Game, Settings, Quit. Keyboard, gamepad and mouse all work.
- **Three save slots** — each shows its room, play time and Stardust. Autosaves at every Starwell, on death, after the boss and on *Save and quit*.
- **Settings** — master / music / effects volume, fullscreen, screen shake, and full key rebinding.
- **Pause menu** — Resume, Settings, Controls, Restart from last Starwell, Save and quit.
- **Camera zones** — corridors hold their height, the wall-jump shaft holds its column, open rooms follow Lune, and the Gatewarden's hall is a fixed wide arena view.
- **New Lune face** — redrawn in a proper three-quarter view.

## How to run

1. Download **`GalaxyCastle_Demo_v0.4_Windows.zip`** below.
2. Extract it — don't run anything from inside the archive.
3. Double-click **`play.bat`**.

Windows 10/11 x64. The first launch joins the four parts of the executable (~110 MB) in a few seconds.

> The build is unsigned, so SmartScreen may warn you: **More info → Run anyway**.

## Known limitations

- Windows only for now.
- Sound effects and music are still placeholders; the art is a work in progress.
