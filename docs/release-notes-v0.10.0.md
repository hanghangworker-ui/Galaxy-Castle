# Galaxy Castle — v0.10.0

The third public build of the demo. The Fallen Gate has grown from five rooms to **thirty-seven**, and roughly **1.5 hours** of content now live in one region.

## Highlights

- **Logical structures, rebuilt** — every stairwell (Climbing Well, Barbican, Starfall Stair) is now a proper stair shaft: ledges anchored in the walls, short platforms hanging on chains. No floating slabs, no step blocks under openings, no one-tile slots.
- **False walls that fool you** — no cracks, no glint; the only tell is *sound*. True stone **rings** under the glaive, false stone **thuds** and cracks. Contents stay rock-textured until the wall breaks, then fade in.
- **Secrets with different rewards** — Star Relic, Comet Ore, a Stardust hoard, stone tablets you press Up at, Heart Fragments and Constellation Shards.
- **Day 8: the Garrison (rooms 26–37)** — Drill Yard, a lever-dropped portcullis shortcut to the Armory, the Sally Port loop, and a new Watch Post with the fourth Orbit Rail station.
- **Two new enemies from data files** — the Husk Brute (big, slow, hits for 2) and the Ember Wisp (fast, fragile, short warning).
- **Two optional challenge rooms** — Thorn Gauntlet (50 tiles of spikes on pillars and hanging platforms) and the Trial Arena (three waves; the vault holds Comet Ore, Stardust and a tablet).
- **Scale:** 37 rooms, 24,753 playable tiles — 8.1× the size of v0.5's Fallen Gate.

1. Download the zip and extract it — don't run the game from inside the zip.
2. Double-click `play.bat`; on first launch it assembles `GalaxyCastle.exe` (~105 MB) from the eleven parts in `data/`.
3. "Windows protected your PC" is expected (the build is unsigned); click **More info → Run anyway**.

Saves live in `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Galaxy Castle\saves\`. Older saves (v0.9 and earlier) load with your stats intact, but you start again at the Fallen Gate — the rooms have moved.
